🎮 Bringing a Classic Back to Life in 3D! 🏗️

Growing up, many of us played the iconic Tower Stack game on old Nokia mobile phones, where precise timing was everything. Recently, I decided to recreate that nostalgic experience with a modern twist by developing a fully 3D version in Unity.

Build the tallest tower in the city in this fun and addictive 3D stacking challenge!
Test your precision as you carefully place building floors one above another. Every perfect placement increases your score and helps your tower reach incredible heights. But be careful—misaligned floors make your tower unstable and can bring your construction crashing down!
Featuring colorful low-poly visuals, smooth gameplay, and simple controls, Tower Stack is easy to learn but challenging to master.

The project features:
✅ 3D low-poly city environment
✅ Physics-based tower stacking
✅ Endless gameplay mechanics
✅ Mobile-friendly controls
✅ Modern visuals while preserving the original game's simplicity and fun

This project was a great exercise in gameplay programming, physics balancing, level design, UI implementation, and optimization.

It's always exciting to take a simple concept that people remember and reimagine it using modern game development techniques.

I would love to hear your feedback and suggestions!
